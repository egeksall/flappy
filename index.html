Movement Controls

  • ⬅️ Left / A / Swipe: Steer Left
  • ➡️ Right / D / Swipe: Steer Right
  • 🔥 Space / Tap / Attack: Dragon Blast
  • ☄️ E / Circle Button: Super Ability (Charge 5 kills for 3s of lane destruction)
  • 📱 On-Screen Touch Buttons: [ ◀ ], [ Attack ], [ ▶ ], [ ⚡ SUPER ]
  • 🛣️ 3 Fixed Lanes: Left | Middle | Right

RPG Dragon Progression

  • ❤️ Health: 500 Dragon HP (Enemies hit for 100+ DMG)
  • ⭐ Level Up: Max Level 20! Choose 1 of 3 Blessings on level up
  • 🐣 1. Baby Form: 100 DMG (Reach Level 5 ➔ Teen)
  • 🐲 Hatchling Ally (Lv 5+): Summons baby dragon companion to auto-attack enemies in your lane!
  • ⚡ 2. Teen Form: 150 DMG (Reach Level 10 ➔ Mature)
  • 👑 3. Mature Form: 200 DMG (Apex Elder Form, Level 20 Cap)
  • 🍖 Sheep: Eat to heal +100 HP and earn +35 EXP
